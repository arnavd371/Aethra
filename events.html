Our Work

Aethra follows the breaks. Seven programmes. 2,340+ participants. 30 countries. Each one starts where one field stops short and another has the missing tool.

  • 01 Flagship

    Moonshot: a 0 to 1 hackathon

    The break is starting from zero. Moonshot is a 0-to-1 build for ideas that do not fit a single field. Teams leave their home discipline, diagnose what is missing, and ship something new instead of refining what already exists. 636 people took part, with support from MongoDB Atlas, CodeCrafters, and Iteration Machine.
