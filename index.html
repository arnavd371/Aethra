We run events where two fields collide.

Aethra organizes events, workshops, and sessions built around one idea: put two different fields in the same room and see what happens. Not a hackathon with a theme bolted on. An actual combination. A Gamethon is not just a hackathon, it is game design plus software engineering, forced into the same 48 hours. A Brandathon is visual identity plus communication. Every event we run works the same way.

We started this way because the most interesting work never stays inside one discipline. Convergence is what we call it when two fields that would not normally talk to each other end up in the same room, and something neither could have made alone comes out of it.

How we do it

  • Events

    Hackathons built around two fields at once instead of one. Gamethon combines game design and software engineering. Brandathon combines visual design and communication. The pairing changes, the format does not.

  • Workshops

    Shorter sessions that teach one discipline through the lens of another, for people who want the crossover without a 48 hour commitment.

  • Sessions

    Smaller, informal gatherings where people from different fields talk through a problem together. No pitch deck required.

What we believe

  • The best ideas live at intersections.

    The most important discoveries happen when two fields that never talked to each other finally do. Not because one borrowed a tool from the other. Because the problem genuinely required both. That is what convergence means. That is what we built Aethra around.

  • Access is the bottleneck.

    Talent is uniformly distributed. Opportunity is not. The next great researcher might be sitting without a mentor, without funding, without a community that takes their questions seriously.

  • Tools shape thought.

    The instruments we build determine the questions we can ask. Better software, better infrastructure, and open models make better science possible.

  • The problems worth solving are hard.

    AI alignment, climate systems, the foundations of mathematics. These are not incremental improvements. They demand full intellectual commitment from the most serious people alive.

  • Community is infrastructure.

    A research community is not a nice-to-have. It is load-bearing. Aethra exists to build that infrastructure: permanent, open, and growing.
