Every Aethra event is built so at least two fields meet on purpose. Across 7 events that is a 100% interdisciplinary format rate: 7 of 7 programmes required people to leave a single-discipline lane.

That produced about 2,340 participant entries into cross-field practice — roughly 334 people per event, and about 78 participants per country reached. If each person brought one home field and engaged a second through the brief, Aethra facilitated at least 2,340 primary–secondary field pairings. Moonshot alone ran 11 categories that paired engineering with other domains.

Published meta-analyses of integrated learning report large gains on critical thinking (standardised mean difference about 1.48) and creativity (about 1.60) versus conventional single-subject instruction. An effect near 1.5 standard deviations moves a typical learner from roughly the middle of a comparison group toward the top tenth. Aethra’s model is built for that shift: repeated, structured contact between fields, at scale, across borders.

Against a world population of about 8.2 billion, cumulative Aethra participation is roughly 1 in 3.5 million people worldwide. The event grid below breaks that reach down event by event.
