Aethra started as an organisation that ran hackathons, ideathons, gamethons, and other events meant to be open and varied in format. We did not fully know what we were building at the start. We figured it out along the way.

In the past few years we organised the events below. Our first six followed that open pattern: Ideathon, Hackathon, Pitchathon, Vibeathon, Gamethon, and Brandathon.

In 2026 we came back with a clearer direction. We launched Moonshot, our first interdisciplinary event and our biggest yet. That is the path we are continuing on.
