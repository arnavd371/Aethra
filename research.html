Research

Research is one of the four things our lab does, alongside events, workshops, and sessions. We are not trying to be a general research organization. We only care about interdisciplinary research, the kind of work that genuinely cannot get done inside one field. In our experience, that is exactly where the most important questions are hiding.

What we support

  • Mentorship, publication, and peer review

    We help connect you with a mentor, support you through publishing your work, and put you in a room with other researchers who take hard questions as seriously as you do. Right now we care most about AI safety, computational biology, climate systems, and the foundations of mathematics, but if your question genuinely needs two fields, we want to hear about it.
